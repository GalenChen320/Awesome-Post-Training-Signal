## Verifiable Rewards

[← Back to main page](../README.md)

Works that obtain rewards **checkable by programs, rules or formal systems**: verifiable task datasets (math, code, proofs, science, instruction constraints), methods to synthesize answers, test cases or checkers, and the design of verifiers themselves. Learned reward models and LLM judges are excluded.
